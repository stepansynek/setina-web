# SETINA site (all pages)

Scope: whole site, index.html first. Mode: Persuade. Audience: machine-shop owners judging the "Web na klíč" offer, through the in-world buyer (purchasing / design engineer with a drawing). Action: send a drawing (drop zone, poptávka) or call a named person. Proof: ranges, tolerances, machines, people, three cases; no invented customers or prices. Constraints: static build.py site, self-hosted fonts and three.js, noindex, fictional-firm note, must look unlike HRANOL (yellow fair stand, Archivo) and stepansynek.com (two-ink export brochure). Pages may be merged: Galerie folds into Zakázky.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is SETINA's measurement protocol. Every claim is a characteristic with nominal, tolerance band and actual, and a tolerance bar shows where your part sits. It refuses the split hero with the render in a grey card, the four-up stat bar, zigzag rows, alternating grey bands and the dark CTA box.

OWN-WORLD: White paper, graphite ink #15181C, nominal blue #2442D6 for every action and nominal value, tolerance green #1A9A55 limits on a pale band #D3EFDD, red #C8283B only for out of range, track grey #EEF0F2. Chivo throughout with tabular figures; 2px ink rules open sections, 1px rules divide rows, 2px corners. Raised from the declined hand: the part size you enter persists across pages and every technology and machine shows fit (chromatophore); sections are solid blocks with real voids, never tinted bands (cloud quarry); every control is a labelled switch with named positions (cassette deck); the inquiry previews what you send before sending (darkroom); spot-ink discipline, no invented tints (risograph).

STORY: A buyer sees a real part measured on arrival, understands the four technologies and their limits in numbers, checks their own part fits, and drops a drawing or calls Lenka. A shop owner sees a site that sells with facts.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header (mark |•| + SETINA, five links, phone, blue "Poslat výkres"). Headline ~80px top-left over eight columns, lead and capacity line right. Full-width band: the pump shaft in orthographic side view, drag spins it on its own axis; six CMM flags below joined by leader lines to its features (M30×2, Ø35 k6, Ø60, Ø45 g6, Ø35 k6, Ø28 h6), each with tolerance, measured value and a tolerance bar. Bottom: working drawing drop zone, primary action.

FORM: Měřicí protokol (CMM inspection report), position 4 of 7 on the grounded list. seed:1e9d1758d566d81f. Signature interaction: drag-to-spin shaft with flag hover highlighting its feature; one authored motion moment, the probe sweep that measures each feature on load.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
